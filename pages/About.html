GET TO KNOW US

"Trust us to capture your memories"

Background

"Who we are"

We are a group of photographers who found joy in capturing the special, or even mundane moments of someone's life. We love what we do, which is why we make ourselves available to everyone who can book our services

Mission

"What we want to do"

We want to share our love of photography. May it be through our services, capturing the client's every moment. Or through giving aspiring photographers an opportunity to improve their skills.

Story

"How we came to be"

Our Founder, Philip Otto Graff often got himself lost while wandering through the city while taking in and capturing the scenery. Getting lost all the time got him to meet a lot of unique individuals who helped him build "Photographic Memories"
